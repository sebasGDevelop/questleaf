MY MUSIC - Questleaf

Put your pieces here (MP3, OGG, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC or OPUS).
The app finds them by itself: they play now and then, like the music the app
composes, and then the silence comes back.

You can also add them from the app: Settings > Sound and music > My music > ADD PIECES.
There you choose whether they play mixed with the app's music or alone.

Use your own music, royalty-free music or music you bought for personal use.
(A folder named "musica" works too.)
